# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in this project to be welcoming, respectful, and free
from harassment for everyone, regardless of background or identity.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and respectful in discussions and reviews.
- Give and receive constructive feedback graciously.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not on people.
- Respect differing viewpoints and keep interactions professional.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, insults, discrimination, personal
attacks, unwanted sexual attention, and publishing another person's private
information without permission.

## Reporting and enforcement

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the project
maintainer privately through GitHub. Please include relevant context or links
and avoid publicizing private information. The maintainer will review reports,
respect confidentiality as far as practical, and take action appropriate to
the situation. Actions may include a warning, removal of content, or exclusion
from project spaces.

This code of conduct applies in project spaces and whenever someone is
representing the project or its community.
